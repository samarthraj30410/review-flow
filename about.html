About ReviewFlo

Building serverless feedback pipelines that keep local storefronts growing without overhead costs.

01

Our Mission

At ReviewFlo, our mission is simple: to configure, brand, and launch high-performance static websites that log customer feedback directly to secure, private spreadsheets — all at zero recurring cost.

We believe small businesses shouldn't have to pay high recurring monthly fees to maintain a private database and collect feedback. That's why we design modern, minimalist review collection tools specifically for local storefronts, cafés, salons, clinics, and gyms.

Zero Maintenance

02

Our Story

ReviewFlo was born from a simple frustration: small business owners were spending $50–$100 every month just to keep a feedback system online. Traditional solutions relied on complex databases and active host instances — an unnecessary burden for a local café or salon.

We asked ourselves: "What if feedback collection could be completely free to run?" By leveraging serverless edge technologies — Vercel CDN, Cloudflare Workers, and Google Sheets API — we built a pipeline that routes review payloads instantaneously and safely, without server databases.

Today, ReviewFlo operates entirely on free-tier hosting, providing local storefronts with a zero-maintenance model that costs nothing to keep online. No servers, no databases, no bills.

Our Core Values

The principles that drive everything we build

Blazing Performance

Sub-second page loads powered by static CDN delivery. Your customers get an instant, frictionless experience — no spinners, no waiting, no abandoned reviews.

Privacy-First Design

Every customer submission flows directly to your private spreadsheet — no third-party databases, no data brokers, no tracking pixels. You own 100% of your data, always.

Zero Overhead Costs

No monthly server bills, no database hosting fees, no hidden charges. Our edge-deployed architecture runs on free-tier infrastructure, saving you $50–$100+ every month compared to traditional solutions.

Radical Simplicity

No admin dashboards to learn, no plugins to install, no CMS to maintain. We hand you a clean, branded static site and a spreadsheet — that's it. If you can read a spreadsheet, you can run ReviewFlo.

Full Code Ownership

Every line of code we write is yours. No vendor lock-in, no proprietary platforms, no subscription traps. Fork it, modify it, host it wherever you want — we transfer complete ownership on delivery.

Always-On Reliability

Static sites don't crash. Deployed across global CDN edge nodes, your feedback page stays online 24/7 with 99.99% uptime — even during traffic surges or server outages that would take down traditional systems.
