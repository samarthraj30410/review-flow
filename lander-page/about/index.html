Our Story

From a humble dream to a beloved community cafe — discover the heart and soul behind every cup we brew.

How It Began

Born from a love
of great coffee

It all started in 2015 when our founders — two baristas with a shared obsession for the perfect cup — decided to create a space that felt like home. They envisioned a cafe where the aroma of freshly roasted beans mingled with laughter, where strangers became friends over cappuccinos, and where every visit left you feeling a little warmer inside.

What began as a tiny corner shop in downtown has blossomed into a beloved destination with multiple locations, but the heart remains the same: exceptional coffee, made with love, served in a space designed for connection.

Our Journey

A decade of
brewing magic

2015

The First Cup

Opened our first tiny cafe with just 8 seats and a dream. Served our first latte to a customer who's still a regular today.

2017

Roasting In-House

Installed our own roaster, giving us full control over flavor profiles and the freshest beans possible.

2019

Second Location

Expanded to a second, larger space with a full kitchen — introducing our beloved pastries and brunch menu.

2022

Community Hub

Launched our events program — open mic nights, art shows, and coffee workshops that bring our community together.

2025

48 Locations & Growing

Now serving thousands daily across 48 locations, with the same warmth and passion we started with.
